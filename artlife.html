Architectural Bureau Artlife

Figma Behance

2025UX/UI conceptweb designdesktop, tablet, mobilefigma, figjam

Artlife is a responsive website concept for a premium architectural bureau working across residential, public and landscape architecture.

The platform brings together the bureau’s services, completed work, standard house projects and a guided project cost calculator.

Choosing an architectural bureau is a complex, high-risk decision. Research showed that clients need proof of expertise, transparent pricing and materials, clear examples of completed work, and predictable communication throughout the project.

Design Process

1/6 Interview Guides

I prepared separate interview scenarios for experienced clients and people choosing an architectural bureau for the first time, so the research could reflect different expectations and decision-making patterns.
